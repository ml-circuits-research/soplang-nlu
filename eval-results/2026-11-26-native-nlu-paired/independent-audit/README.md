# Independent audit of the superseded native review pilot

The audit uses the frozen native predictions in this benchmark directory. It evaluates a previous review rubric that was superseded before all primary model judgments could finish. Its grades and disagreement figures do not enter the benchmark’s final grade counts.

GPT-6.2-Sol independently reviewed forty groups selected without reading the primary GPT-6-Luna grades. Four deterministic strata emphasized fully accounted candidate graphs carrying roles and attachments, inferred actions, and optional classifications, with a general comparison stratum. Candidate-set membership and original English wording were preserved. The selection manifest records exact packet identity and quotas. The overlap report uses only completed primary checkpoints.

The disagreement rows compare the actual chosen candidate circuits. For example, the pilot says that row 313 candidate 4 omits the blue jacket and the destination, though the circuit attaches the jacket to the boy and the destination to the movement clause. A separate blinded repeat selected the same circuit for L8. The pilot says that row 1174 candidate 0 uses “ok” as a subject and “thanks” as part of an object, though its sole predicate is “remove,” its direct object is “every node,” and both discourse words are filler.

Separate structural diagnostics identify cases where every eligible graph explicitly mislabels a sentence-initial auxiliary–subject inversion and cases where every graph encodes a contracted personal pronoun as a name. They inspect the frozen native circuits directly and do not assign or replace grades.
