# Scope of the archived review

The inputs and emitted w.request candidates come from the parent run. The historical evaluation used 1,861 row IDs and three strategies. The batch reviewer selected witnesses from each distinct group of structurally eligible candidates. A second pass checked inconsistencies between a rationale and its grade. Independent confirmation checked proposed L10 witnesses against all supplied references. The compressed artifacts retain the original decisions, stricter judgments, confirmations, alternative-witness reviews and the final per-engine grades.

These are model-assisted semantic judgments. The mechanical WebMeet comparator and the review measure different criteria. Exact text coverage, syntactic role assignment and end-to-end task execution require separate evaluation. The new nlu schema and native strategy parsers were introduced after these predictions.
