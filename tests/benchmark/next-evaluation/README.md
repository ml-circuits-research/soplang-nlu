# Prospective evaluation of up to two public readings

The primary outcome is the percentage of original English messages that receive at least one plausible public native SOP circuit, with at most two circuits returned. Score the nonempty public set without regard to order: mixed successful sets are L7 and wholly successful sets are L8–L10. Empty sets and sets with no plausible interpretation remain failures.

Before any judgment, use the actual schema and full-source validator to screen every public candidate. For a plausible circuit, the meaningful source words have grounded roles, explicit quantities and values are preserved, independent speech acts are represented, and participants and qualified concepts connect to their clauses. An embedded clause or a qualified noun phrase can have its own wire. Source-grounded pronouns, unresolved contextual reference and reasonable alternate attachments are permissible. Semantic world classification is optional. An incidental stylistic, punctuation or nonessential role-label difference does not invalidate a structurally plausible whole reading. A material omitted clause, missing value, wrong attachment, or quoted span that hides a missing structural analysis still fails. A graph can be judged only as returned; reviewers cannot stitch two partial readings together.

The prospective protocol pins the population and prior exposures. It draws 1,831 unique texts from unexposed committed rows, using a fresh seed and an ordered hash of the row ID. The size is 1% of all 183,094 rows, rounded. Both learned weights and runtime retrieval must exclude every reserved exact source text. Exact UTF-8 digests preserve Unicode and case. Keep this cohort out of development, checkpoint selection and grammar design. The training process can check the digest list without loading held-out texts. Use the existing two independent blind passes and adjudicate all acceptance disagreements. Re-run reference and source checks against the pinned revision.

The [November comparison](../../../eval-results/2026-11-26-fresh-locked-one-percent/README.md) predates the two-reading limit. On its 1,831 sources, retaining each engine's first two already-public readings and reusing their settled individual verdicts yields 72.69% ANY for Neuro-Selector, 64.77% for spaCy Beam and 60.51% for spaCy Direct. The original selector returned up to three and achieved 74.99%. These post-hoc figures are development baselines; a new confirmation uses the reserved cohort.
