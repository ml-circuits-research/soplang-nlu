# Prolog

The native reader indexes vocabulary and frame declarations from the configured installation and additional worldPaths. It sends token forms and lexical possibilities to its persistent SWI-Prolog process. The Prolog grammar selects clause boundaries, predicate and argument roles, entity groups, coordination, scope and attachment alternatives. The strategy serializes these solutions as compact NLU circuits. Its normal analysis call skips the older WebMeet request generator.

A syntactic role can survive without an associated world concept. Literal pronouns remain on their own entity wires. Unknown spans stay on the clause or entity as unresolved evidence. Optional semantic annotations carry an evidence record identifying their wire, key, source and binding. Inspect the full alternatives when judging ambiguous attachments.

Supply a SWI-Prolog executable via swipl, or install swipl on PATH. The symbolic strategy accepts no training input. The original hybrid reader also offered retrieval of exact labelled examples, template instantiation and transfer from similar examples. Its historical timings and scores belong to that different implementation.

The [Prolog example-transfer strategy](prolog-example-transfer.md) adds supervised generalisation as a distinct implementation. Its training split and artifact belong to that strategy.
