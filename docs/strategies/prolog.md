# Prolog

At startup the strategy loads SOP schemas, language frames, vocabulary and semantic bindings from the configured knowledge installation and additional worldPaths. Node builds the indexes used to identify text spans. SWI-Prolog matches candidate predicates, targets and constraints, and the Node reader composes and validates the resulting circuits. Readings retain their own evidence and catalog diagnostics. An utterance can produce several candidates or leave spans unresolved.

Supply a SWI-Prolog executable via swipl, or install swipl on PATH. The symbolic strategy accepts no training input. The original hybrid reader also offered retrieval of exact labelled examples, template instantiation and transfer from similar examples. Its historical timings and scores belong to that different implementation.

A future example-trained strategy could generalise roles across labelled sentences. Its model, training split and evaluation split would belong to that strategy. To measure generalisation, keep the evaluated texts disjoint from the training examples, record overlap after normalisation and report separately how many paraphrase families cross the split.
